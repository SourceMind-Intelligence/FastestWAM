## 3. Decisions worth carrying over

1. **Always save full training state.** The Sep 17 pilot lost its optimizer state; since Sep 28 every run saves weights plus Accelerate/DeepSpeed state every 1,000 steps and keeps two. `trainer_state.json` is the completion marker.
2. **A resume keeps its GPU topology.** The 7-GPU run is not continued on 8 GPUs; a different card count is a new run.
3. **GPU 6 is a normal card** unless a failure can be reproduced by hand (Sep 28).
4. **No further same-recipe MIP training wave for now.** C's gain is 19/2,000 at 1.73× training time; visual-shift robustness is the larger shared weakness.
5. **Compare trained-policy packages honestly.** Equal steps is not equal compute; XM vs FM needs the same patched code, warm start, 8 GPUs, seed and schedule, reported at equal steps and at equal compute.
6. **MIP details that matter.** The proposal and Tengfei's code use the paper form (train on 0.9·A + 0.1·z, infer from 0.9·Â₀). `mixed` and `pred` modes feed a detached first prediction into the second pass. MIP inference is a fixed two passes regardless of the `--denoise-steps` flag.
7. **Compact action head needs real architecture work**, with matched accuracy and latency, before any efficiency claim.
8. **Research framing** (Sep 18 and Sep 30): three questions, observation/encoder, training/interface, inference/budget; training-method ideas (MIP, XM, self-forcing) are kept separate from model-organization ideas (DoT-style shallow action head, future caching).
9. **Server boundaries.** Training changes only in `/root/evan/Fastest-WAM-evan/`; Tengfei's directory is read-only except for running his existing evaluator; other users' directories untouched.
